MacBook Pro 3D · macOS Tilt & Fold

Native SwiftUI App

Interactive 3D MacBook Pro Studio

A web-based 3D MacBook Pro visualization with real-time fold and tilt physics, built with Three.js and WebGL. Explore the high-resolution anodized aluminum chassis, keyboard well, glass trackpad, and vivid macOS display.

Key Interactive Features

  • Lid Tilt & Fold Slider: Smoothly adjust the display angle from 0° (closed shut) to 135° (wide viewing angle).
  • Auto Play Animation: Watch the MacBook lid gently open and close in a smooth breathing cycle.
  • macOS Desktop UI: Dynamic 2560x1600 Retina screen with real-time clock, Apple menu bar, desktop shortcuts, and authentic glass dock with app icons.
  • High-Resolution Wallpapers: Switch between official Apple Park, Blue Waves, Sunflower, and Wallhaven landscapes, or upload custom wallpapers.
  • Controls & Hotkeys: Drag to orbit 360°, scroll to zoom, Space to play/pause, Arrow keys to adjust angle, R to reset view.
